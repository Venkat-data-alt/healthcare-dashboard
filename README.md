🏥 Healthcare Patient Analytics Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

The Healthcare Patient Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution developed to analyze hospital performance across key operational and financial metrics. The dashboard provides insights into patient volume, revenue generation, patient satisfaction, departmental performance, doctor performance, and readmission trends.

The objective of this project is to help hospital management make data-driven decisions that improve patient care, operational efficiency, and overall business performance.

🎯 Business Problem

Hospitals generate large amounts of patient and operational data daily. However, extracting meaningful insights from this data can be challenging.

Management requires visibility into:

Patient volume and demographics
Revenue performance
Department performance
Doctor performance
Patient satisfaction
Readmission rates
Insurance contribution

This dashboard centralizes these metrics into a single analytical solution.

📖 Storytelling & Business Insights
Story

Over the analyzed period, the hospital served 1,500 patients and generated approximately ₹77 million in revenue.

The dashboard reveals that ICU and Oncology departments contribute significantly to overall hospital revenue, making them key drivers of financial performance.

Patient satisfaction averages 3 out of 5, indicating opportunities for service quality improvements.

Readmission analysis highlights conditions such as Parkinson's Disease and Heart Failure as major contributors to repeat admissions, suggesting the need for stronger follow-up care programs.

Revenue trends indicate a gradual decline across months, which may require further investigation into patient volume, treatment demand, or operational challenges.

Overall, the dashboard provides management with actionable insights to improve healthcare outcomes while maintaining financial sustainability.
