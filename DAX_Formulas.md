# DAX Functions Used in Healthcare Patient Analytics Dashboard

This document contains the most important DAX formulas used to solve the business problem statements in the Healthcare Patient Analytics Dashboard.

---

# Measures

These measures were used to calculate business KPIs and support decision-making.

## Total Patients

### Business Purpose
Used to measure the total number of patients treated by the hospital.

```DAX
Total Patients =
DISTINCTCOUNT(Healthcare[PatientID])
```

---

## Total Revenue

### Business Purpose
Used to calculate the total revenue generated by the hospital.

```DAX
Total Revenue =
SUM(Healthcare[TreatmentCostINR])
```

---

## Average Satisfaction Score

### Business Purpose
Used to analyse overall patient satisfaction and service quality.

```DAX
Avg Satisfaction =
AVERAGE(Healthcare[SatisfactionScore])
```

---

## Average Length of Stay

### Business Purpose
Used to evaluate patient stay duration and hospital resource utilisation.

```DAX
Avg Length Of Stay =
AVERAGE(Healthcare[LengthOfStayDays])
```

---

## Readmitted Patients

### Business Purpose
Used to identify the number of patients readmitted within 30 days.

```DAX
Readmitted Patients =
CALCULATE(
    COUNTROWS(Healthcare),
    Healthcare[Readmitted30Days] = "Yes"
)
```

---

## Readmission Rate %

### Business Purpose
Used to measure the percentage of patients returning to the hospital.

```DAX
Readmission Rate =
DIVIDE(
    [Readmitted Patients],
    [Total Patients]
)
```

---

## Revenue Per Patient

### Business Purpose
Used to evaluate financial performance at the patient level.

```DAX
Revenue Per Patient =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Revenue],
    [Total Patients]
)
```

---

## Previous Month Revenue

### Business Purpose
Used to compare current revenue against the previous month.

```DAX
Previous Month Revenue =
CALCULATE(
    [Total Revenue],
    PREVIOUSMONTH(Dim_Date[Date])
)
```

---

## Revenue Growth %

### Business Purpose
Used to monitor month-over-month revenue growth.

```DAX
Revenue Growth % =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Revenue] - [Previous Month Revenue],
    [Previous Month Revenue]
)
```

---

## YTD Revenue

### Business Purpose
Used to analyse cumulative revenue from the beginning of the year.

```DAX
YTD Revenue =
TOTALYTD(
    [Total Revenue],
    Dim_Date[Date]
)
```

# Calculated Columns

Calculated columns were created to categorise and enrich the existing data.

## Age Group

### Business Purpose
Used for demographic analysis and patient segmentation.

```DAX
Age Group =
SWITCH(
    TRUE(),
    Dim_Patient[Age] <= 18, "Children",
    Dim_Patient[Age] <= 40, "Young Adults",
    Dim_Patient[Age] <= 60, "Adults",
    "Senior Citizens"
)
```

---

# Tables

Additional tables were created to improve data modelling and enable time intelligence functions.

## Date Table

### Business Purpose
Used for monthly, quarterly, yearly, and trend analysis.

```DAX
Dim_Date =
ADDCOLUMNS(
    CALENDAR(
        MIN(Healthcare[AdmissionDate]),
        MAX(Healthcare[AdmissionDate])
    ),
    "Year", YEAR([Date]),
    "Month Number", MONTH([Date]),
    "Month Name", FORMAT([Date], "MMM"),
    "Quarter", "Q" & FORMAT([Date], "Q")
)
```
