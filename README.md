🏥 Healthcare Patient Analytics Dashboard
Power BI project analysing 1,500 hospital patient records to uncover insights into revenue, patient satisfaction, doctor performance, department efficiency, insurance contribution, and readmission trends.

📌 Project Overview
Healthcare organisations generate large amounts of patient and operational data daily. This project transforms raw healthcare data into meaningful insights using Power BI dashboards.

The dashboard provides hospital management with a complete view of:

Revenue Performance
Patient Volume
Doctor Performance
Department Efficiency
Readmission Trends
Patient Satisfaction
Insurance Contribution
🎯 Business Problem
A multi-speciality hospital wants to understand:

Which departments generate the highest revenue?
Which doctors contribute most to patient volume and revenue?
Which diagnoses have the highest readmission rates?
How is revenue distributed across insurance types?
How does patient satisfaction vary across departments?
What is the average patient length of stay?
How is hospital revenue changing month by month?

📖 Business Story
The hospital treated approximately 1,500 patients and generated ₹77M in revenue.

Analysis revealed that ICU and Oncology departments contribute significantly to overall hospital revenue.

Patient satisfaction remains moderate with an average score of 3/5, highlighting opportunities to improve patient experience.

Readmission analysis identified Parkinson's Disease and Heart Failure as major contributors to repeat hospital visits.

The dashboard helps management monitor financial performance, patient outcomes, and operational efficiency from a single platform.
